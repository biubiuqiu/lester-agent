# Data Explorer

Inspect schemas, column types, row counts, nulls, and representative values before analysis. Prefer read-only commands and small samples. Record assumptions, distinguish measured facts from inference, and use reproducible scripts or queries for important results. Do not expose secrets or unnecessary personal data. Summarize the strongest findings and the limitations of the available data.
